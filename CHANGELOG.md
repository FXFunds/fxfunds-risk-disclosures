# Changelog

All notable changes to this repository are recorded here.

## [1.0.0] — 2026-10-19

### Added
- Official document register with 16 entries across General, Trading, Payments, Crypto, Legal and Privacy (`register.json` and `docs/document-register.md`).
- Risk categories table: market, leverage, digital asset, stablecoin, blockchain, operational, counterparty and regulatory risk.
- Trading risk disclosures by market category, with a leverage and margin table.
- Cryptocurrency and stablecoin risk guide.
- Deposit and withdrawal terms checklist.
- Legal, regulatory and privacy guide, including how to check regulation yourself.
- Maintenance policy, ReadTheDocs documentation and links to related FXFunds repositories.
- Node.js package `@fxfunds/risk-disclosures` and Python package `fxfunds-risk-disclosures` with the `fxfunds-risk` CLI.
- Datasets `dataset/fxfunds_document_register.csv` and `dataset/fxfunds_risk_categories.csv` for Hugging Face and Kaggle, with a Kaggle notebook.
- Consistency tests and GitHub Actions workflows for tests, npm and PyPI publishing.

### Register status at this release
- REG-01 Official website: located (https://fxfunds.com/).
- REG-02 to REG-16: not located on publicly readable pages or by public web search.
