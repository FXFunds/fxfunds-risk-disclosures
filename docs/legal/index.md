# Legal, Regulatory and Privacy Information

At the last check (2026-10-19), the following were **not located** on FXFunds' publicly readable pages: terms and conditions, client agreement, operator company name and address, licence or regulator details, complaints procedure, AML/KYC policy, privacy policy and cookie policy. See register entries REG-10 to REG-16.

**Do not infer that a platform is authorised from claims on its website or from the presence of legal pages.** Check with the regulator directly.

## Operator Identity

The terms should name the company you contract with, its registration number and its registered address. If these are missing, you may not know which company holds your money or which laws apply.

## How to Check Regulation Yourself

1. Find the operator's **exact legal name** and **registration or licence number** in the official terms.
2. Go to the regulator's **own website** (type the address yourself) and search its public register.
3. Check that the register entry lists the **same company name, website domain and permitted services**.
4. Watch for **clone firms**: fraudsters copy the details of a real authorised firm. Contact the firm using the details on the regulator's register, not those on the website.
5. Check whether the regulator lists the platform on any **warning or alert list**.

Examples of public registers and warning lists:

| Country / region | Regulator | Resource |
|------------------|-----------|----------|
| United Kingdom | FCA | Financial Services Register and warning list |
| United States | CFTC / NFA | NFA BASIC member search |
| Cyprus | CySEC | Regulated entities and warnings |
| Australia | ASIC | Professional registers and Moneysmart investor alerts |
| India | RBI / SEBI | RBI Alert List of unauthorised forex trading platforms; SEBI registered intermediaries |

Being registered in one country does not mean a platform may serve clients in another. Check that the authorisation covers your country and the products you plan to use.

## Complaints

The terms should explain how to make a complaint and whether an independent dispute-resolution body or compensation scheme applies. If no complaints process is published, ask for it in writing before depositing.

## Privacy and Data Protection

A privacy policy should explain:

- what personal data is collected (for example identity documents, contact details, payment and device data);
- why it is used and on what legal basis;
- who it is shared with, including payment providers and identity-verification services;
- whether it is transferred to other countries;
- how long it is kept and how it is protected;
- how to access, correct or delete your data, and whom to contact.

## Identity Verification (KYC)

Regulated platforms usually verify identity before allowing deposits or withdrawals. Only upload identity documents through the official platform on https://fxfunds.com/, never through links in unexpected messages or to people claiming to be support staff.
