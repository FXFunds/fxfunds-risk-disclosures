## Regulatory risk

**Is FXFunds registered with the U.S. SEC?**

FXFUNDS LTD appears in SEC EDGAR records under CIK 0002137376 following a Form D filing. A Form D is a securities-offering notice, not a brokerage licence or SEC endorsement.

**Does an SEC filing mean FXFunds is approved by the SEC?**

No. The SEC's acceptance of a Form D filing does not indicate endorsement or approval of the company, its investment products or its trading platform.

**Does FXFunds have a FinCEN registration?**

FXFunds has supplied documentation describing a FinCEN money services business registration. An MSB registration concerns activities within the applicable money services framework and does not, by itself, authorise securities, forex brokerage or derivatives trading.

**Is FXFunds regulated internationally?**

Regulatory permissions depend on the specific operating entity, jurisdiction and financial services involved. Applicable licences and their scope should be identified through official regulatory records.
