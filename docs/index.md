# FXFunds Risk Disclosure Bot

An informational project that explains the risks of online trading, leverage, cryptocurrency transactions and account security, and keeps a register of official FXFunds risk disclosures and legal documents. Its purpose is to make risk information accessible, encourage careful verification and support informed decisions.
